01 / WHO WE ARE

A LITTLE ABOUT US

We are the people
behind the moments.

WE is a student organisation responsible for social activities within SITAM — the IT Faculty of SDU University.

Our mission is to make student life more exciting, meaningful and connected. Through events, gatherings and creative initiatives, we bring students together and make university life unforgettable.

We believe that university is not only about studying. It is also about people, friendships, experiences and memories that stay with us.
